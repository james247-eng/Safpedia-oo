Our Mission

Why we exist

Founded in Nigeria, SAFpedia exists to close the gap between wanting a better income and knowing where to start. Every member gets the same three doors in: a course worth finishing, a storefront worth building, and a referral link worth sharing — under one account, one login.

Learning that pays for itself

Courses and ebooks priced so a first sale or two covers the cost.

Support that doesn't end at checkout

Help is there after your first course, first sale, and first payout — not just before.

A community, not just a platform

Members across the continent learning, selling, and referring side by side.

What Makes Us Different

You're never stuck alone
