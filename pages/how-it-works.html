Common Questions

Still have questions?

The most common ones, answered directly.

Do I have to pick just one — learning, selling, or earning?

No. One account does all three — enrol in a course, list a product, and share your referral link at the same time.

How fast do I actually get paid?

Confirmed sales and commissions are available for withdrawal within 24 hours, straight to your linked bank account.

What if I don't have a product to sell yet?

That's fine — start with a course or your referral link. You can open a storefront whenever you're ready.

Is there a fee to join SAFpedia?

Creating an account is free. Course and platform fees are shown upfront on the pricing page before you pay for anything.

Can I sell to buyers outside Nigeria?

Yes — SAFpedia supports buyers across Africa, with local payment methods for supported countries.
