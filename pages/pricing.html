Common Questions

About subscriptions

The details people usually ask about.

Can I pay monthly or annually?

Yes. SafBloom and SafScale support monthly and discounted annual billing. Safseed is free for life.

Do vendors pay commission on product sales?

No. Subscription fees are the platform revenue mechanism, and vendors keep 100% of each product sale.

What happens when my subscription expires?

New product listing is paused and subscription-lapsed listings are deactivated until you renew.

What if a buyer requests a refund?

Refunds and disputes are handled through the marketplace support process.

Which tier should I choose?

Choose based on the number of active products and the support features your storefront needs.
