Common Questions

About the fee

The details people usually ask about.

Is there a monthly or yearly subscription?

No. Joining and listing are free indefinitely. The only charge is the 10% fee, and only when a sale actually completes.

When exactly is the 10% deducted?

Immediately after a successful checkout. The fee is recorded against that sale automatically — you never get billed separately.

Does the fee apply to referral commissions I pay out?

The 10% platform fee is calculated on the sale total. Any referral commission you've set comes out of your remaining share, not on top of it.

What if a buyer requests a refund?

If a sale is refunded, the platform fee tied to that sale is reversed too — you're not left covering a fee on money you no longer have.

Is the 10% different for courses vs marketplace products?

No — it's a flat 10% across courses, ebooks, and marketplace listings, so there's nothing to calculate differently by category.
