Privacy Policy

SAFpedia collects account, order, payment and support information needed to operate the marketplace. We use it to authenticate users, process orders, support vendors, prevent fraud and communicate service updates.

Payment card details are handled by our payment processor and are not stored by SAFpedia. We retain marketplace records for legal, security and service purposes. You may request access, correction or deletion of personal information by contacting support.

We use reasonable safeguards and disclose information only to service providers, payment partners, authorities where required, or parties involved in a transaction. This policy may be updated as the service evolves.
